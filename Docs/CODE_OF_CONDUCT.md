# Code of Conduct

We want a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected Behaviour
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers and differing viewpoints.
- Give and accept feedback graciously.
- Respect privacy: never share confidential or personal data.

## Unacceptable Behaviour
- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks
- Sexualised language or unwelcome attention
- Publishing others' private information
- Disruptive or bad-faith conduct

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation. Report concerns to _<contact email>_. Reports are handled confidentially.

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant concepts.
